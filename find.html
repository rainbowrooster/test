Find In Stores

Support your local independent booksellers and save on shipping by picking up your copy of Rainbow Rooster Colors O'ahu near you.

Apple Maps Guide Google Maps Guide

Hawai'i

Sugarcane

Kaimuki, O'ahu

A curated collection of gifts, jewelry, and local products celebrating island culture and style.

The Growing Keiki

Hale'iwa, O'ahu

A beloved children's and gifts boutique featuring toys, books, and locally-inspired products for keiki of all ages.

Tini Manini

Hale'iwa, O'ahu

A boutique featuring clothing, jewelry, and gifts with a focus on local and sustainable products.

U.S. Mainland

Aloha Northwest

Kirkland, WA
Edmonds, WA

Aloha Northwest is a breath of fresh aloha on gray Seattle days. Tanya's stores feature beautiful jewelry, unique island gifts, and of course, awesome keiki sections. Find your way here and you'll be transported to the islands and find something perfect to bring home.

Elliott Bay Book Company

Seattle, WA

Our favorite Seattle independent bookstore featuring not only a broad selection of books but also booksellers who love their work. We are so proud to be represented here.

Hawaii General Store

Seattle, WA

Hawaii General Store feels like Gail has literally lifted and transported the store directly from the islands. You'll find all the essentials here including Dole Whip, crackseed, aloha attire, and even Hawaiian breads.

Banter Bookshop

Fremont, CA

Amy has built Fremont's independent bookstore into a true community center. We love their kids area and their regular community events.
