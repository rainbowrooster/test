Product Highlights

✓ A Fresh Take on the Travel Guide: A one-of-a-kind hybrid combining insights of a great travel guidebook with the fun of coloring books, perfect for tourists and locals alike.

✓ What Makes It Special: Written from the perspective of a 10-year-old co-author, readers follow Rainbow Rooster to 28 attractions on the island. Hand-drawn illustrations invite personalization through coloring, with a fun "egg hunt" challenging readers to find 18 Hawaiian symbols.

✓ Broad Market Appeal: Serves as a pre-trip planner, meaningful souvenir, or thoughtful gift for families seeking authentic travel experiences and meaningful connections to Hawai'i.
