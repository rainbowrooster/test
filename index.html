What Makes This Travel Guide Special

Written by a local to give you an authentic experience that engages, entertains, and (hopefully) educates travelers to O'ahu

💎

Hidden Gems

Discover secret spots that only locals know. From uncrowded beaches to hiking trails frequented by locals only, explore O'ahu beyond the tourist path with insider recommendations to enjoy O'ahu as locals do.

🍜

Best Local Eats

Taste the real flavors of O'ahu with our curated guide to authentic plate lunches, shave ice, poke bowls, and local favorites. Every recommendation is a local staple worth seeking out.

📖

Not Boring O'ahu Facts

Instead of learning about O'ahu's history through a historical narrative, you'll instead find answers to questions like why you don't see any shield volcanoes on O'ahu.

✏️

Hand-Drawn Illustrations

Every location features original artwork that captures the natural beauty and whimsical spirit of O'ahu. Each illustration combines careful attention to architectural and landscape details with imaginative touches that bring the island's magic to life.

🎨

Color Your Journey

Each attraction features a beautiful illustration that invites you to color them in with your experiences and your own perspectives as you explore the island.

🎲

Fun Activities

As you color, hunt for Hawaiian symbols hidden throughout the illustrations, compete to score the most points in a travel scavenger hunt, and answer fun prompts for your travel journal.

🌺

Learn About Hawai'i

Pick up some common Hawaiian words and explore engaging sections on the island's unique geology, rich history, vibrant culture, and the people who call Hawai'i home.

Perfect for families, solo travelers, and everyone in between.
