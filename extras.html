Book Extras

Beautiful photos of featured locations, puzzle solutions, and bonus resources to enhance your O'ahu adventure.

🏖️

Lanikai Beach

One of O'ahu's most stunning beaches with turquoise waters and the iconic Mokulua Islands in the background. Perfect for kayaking and sunrise photos.

⛰️

Diamond Head

The volcanic crater that defines Honolulu's skyline. Hike to the summit for panoramic views of Waikīkī and the Pacific Ocean.

🍜

Local Plate Lunch

The quintessential Hawaiian meal featuring two scoops rice, macaroni salad, and your choice of protein. This is what locals eat every day.

🌊

North Shore Waves

World-famous surfing destination. Winter brings massive waves that attract professional surfers from around the globe.

🐢

Honu (Sea Turtle)

These gentle creatures are sacred in Hawaiian culture. Remember to admire them from a respectful distance.

🍧

Shave Ice

Not shaved ice—shave ice! This Hawaiian treat features finely shaved ice with colorful syrups and optional ice cream at the bottom.

Rooster's Riddle

Page 18: "I'm tall and shaped like a diamond, locals climb me at dawn, and from my peak you can see the whole town. What am I?"

Answer: Diamond Head! Also known as Lē'ahi in Hawaiian. The best time to hike is early morning before it gets too hot. Don't forget to bring water!

Map Challenge

Page 24: Connect the dots to reveal a famous O'ahu landmark!

Answer: The completed image shows the Aloha Tower, Honolulu's historic lighthouse and clock tower. Built in 1926, it was once the tallest building in Hawaii!

Food Trivia

Page 31: Match each local food to its description!

Answers:
1. Poke → Fresh raw fish marinated in soy sauce and sesame oil
2. Loco Moco → Rice topped with hamburger patty, fried egg, and gravy
3. Malasada → Portuguese donut without a hole, rolled in sugar
4. Spam Musubi → Rice and Spam wrapped in nori seaweed
5. Haupia → Coconut milk-based Hawaiian dessert

Color Code

Page 45: Use the color key to decode the secret message!

Decoded Message: "E komo mai" — This means "Welcome" in Hawaiian! It's what you'll hear when you arrive at someone's home or enter a local business. The spirit of aloha is all about welcoming others with open arms.
